## The options compared

| Option | Neighbouring teeth | Surgery on the bone | Removable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implant | untouched | yes | no |
| Resin-bonded bridge | little or no drilling | no | no |
| Bridge | drilled down | no | no |
| Partial denture | held by clasps or telescopes | no | yes |

### Resin-bonded bridge

The artificial tooth is attached to a neighbouring tooth with a thin wing. Very gentle and well suited for a missing incisor – in selected cases also for back teeth. See [resin-bonded bridge](/en/services/resin-bonded-bridge/).

### Conventional bridge

The neighbouring teeth receive crowns and the replacement tooth sits between them. Useful if the neighbouring teeth are already damaged. A bridge is fixed and feels like your own teeth.

### Partial denture

A removable restoration held by clasps or telescopic crowns on your own teeth. Good if several teeth are missing in different places, or as a temporary solution.

## How we advise you

1. We examine the gap, the neighbouring teeth, the gums and your bite.
2. We explain which solutions are possible in your situation – with pros and cons, durability and costs.
3. You decide in your own time. If you wish, you can take the cost plan home to compare.

## Duration, durability and care

Resin-bonded bridges and bridges are usually finished in two to three visits, a partial denture in four to six. All solutions need thorough cleaning between the teeth and regular check-ups. A denture is taken out for cleaning.

## Costs

- **Statutory insurance (GKV):** the insurer pays a **fixed subsidy (Festzuschuss)** based on the diagnosis – regardless of which solution you choose. With a complete **bonus booklet** it rises by 10 or 15 percentage points. You see your **own share** in advance in the treatment and cost plan.
- **Private insurance and self-payers:** charged according to the German fee scale for dentists (GOZ).
