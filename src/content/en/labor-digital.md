## Our equipment

- **Intraoral scanner:** digital impressions without impression paste
- **CAD/CAM:** computer-aided design and manufacturing of restorations
- **3D implant planning:** implants planned on screen, with a surgical guide if useful
- **Digital X-rays:** images available immediately, with a low radiation dose
- **3D X-ray imaging (CBCT):** [[PRÜFEN: in der Praxis vorhanden oder per Überweisung?]]

## How a restoration is made

1. **Scan:** we record your teeth digitally.
2. **Design:** dentist and master technician plan shape, material and shade together.
3. **Production:** the restoration is milled or printed and then finished by hand.
4. **Fine-tuning:** if needed, we check shade and shape with you beforehand.
5. **Fitting:** the finished restoration is checked and fitted.
