## When does an implant make sense?

- **One tooth is missing:** the implant replaces it without drilling down the neighbouring teeth.
- **Several teeth are missing:** implants can carry a fixed bridge instead of a removable partial denture.
- **All teeth are missing:** just two to four implants can give a denture firm hold. With more implants, a fixed bridge is possible.
- **Your denture is loose:** implants clearly improve the hold, especially in the lower jaw.

An implant is not always the best solution. For young people who are still growing, with certain illnesses or medications, or if bone grafting should be avoided, there are good [alternatives](/en/services/implant-alternatives/).

## How the treatment works

1. **Consultation and diagnosis:** we examine teeth, gums and bone. If needed, we take a 3D X-ray (cone beam CT, CBCT). [[PRÜFEN: DVT in der Praxis oder Überweisung?]]
2. **3D planning:** on the computer we plan the position, length and angle of the implant so that the crown will sit correctly later. If useful, we make a surgical guide for a very precise procedure.
3. **Placing the implant:** under local anaesthetic. For a single implant, this usually takes less than an hour.
4. **Healing:** the implant fuses with the bone. This usually takes two to four months, longer with bone grafting. In the meantime you usually wear a temporary tooth.
5. **Restoration:** after healing we scan the implant digitally. Our lab makes the crown, bridge or denture.
6. **Aftercare:** regular check-ups and professional cleaning protect the implant in the long term.

## Immediate restoration

Sometimes an implant can be placed directly after a tooth is removed (immediate implant placement). With enough firm bone, a temporary crown or bridge on the same day is also possible (immediate restoration). This does not work for everyone. We tell you honestly whether the conditions are met. ## Alternatives

- [Resin-bonded bridge](/en/services/resin-bonded-bridge/) for a missing front tooth
- Conventional bridge if the neighbouring teeth need crowns anyway
- Partial or full denture
- Leaving the gap if chewing and appearance are not affected

## Duration and number of visits

From the first consultation to the finished tooth usually takes three to six months, longer with bone grafting. Expect five to eight visits.

## Durability and care

Implants cannot get caries, but the gums around them can become inflamed. Brush thoroughly, clean between teeth with interdental brushes and come for regular check-ups and professional cleaning. Smoking clearly increases the risk of inflammation.

## Costs

- **Statutory insurance (GKV):** the insurer usually pays nothing for the implant and the surgery. It does pay the **fixed subsidy (Festzuschuss)** for the crown or bridge, as if you had chosen the standard solution (for example a bridge). With a complete **bonus booklet (Bonusheft)** the subsidy rises to 70 or 75 percent of the standard solution. Exceptions apply only in particularly severe cases, such as congenital malformations or after tumour surgery.
- **Private insurance:** charged according to the German fee scale for dentists (GOZ). Many policies reimburse implants fully or partly.
- **Supplementary dental insurance:** many policies cover part of the cost. Check waiting periods and maximum amounts first.

**New to Germany?** With an international or travel policy you usually pay the bill and claim it back; ask your insurer before treatment. Read more in [Seeing a dentist in Berlin](/en/guide/dentist-berlin-german-system/).

A single implant with a crown usually costs around [[PREIS]] euros in total at our practice, depending on the bone and material. You receive a written cost plan in advance.
