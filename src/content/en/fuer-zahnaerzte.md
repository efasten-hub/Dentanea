## What you can refer for

- **Complex prosthodontics:** full-mouth rehabilitations, increasing the vertical dimension, telescopic and hybrid prosthetics, replica dentures
- **Implants and implant prosthodontics:** planning, placement, bone grafting, restoration – including individual steps only
- **Resin-bonded bridges:** single- and two-wing, anterior and selected posterior cases
- **Children and teenagers:** congenitally missing teeth, peg-shaped teeth, tooth loss after trauma, interim restorations coordinated with orthodontics
- **Inherited hard-tissue disorders:** MIH, amelogenesis imperfecta, dentinogenesis imperfecta, syndromic findings
- **Functional diagnosis and therapy:** TMD, splint therapy, mandibular advancement devices
- **Second opinion:** on planning, prognosis or choice of material

## Process

1. **Referral:** informally by letter, fax or email with your question. X-rays and findings in advance are welcome – please encrypted or via KIM. [[PRÜFEN: KIM-Adresse der Praxis]]
2. **Appointment:** your patient books by phone or online. We get in touch if treatment is urgent.
3. **Examination and planning:** we discuss the approach and, if needed, the division of tasks with you.
4. **Treatment:** only within the agreed scope.
5. **Report:** you receive a written report with findings, treatment and recommendations for aftercare.
