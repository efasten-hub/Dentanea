## How we work

- **Examine thoroughly before planning.** Many problems have more than one cause, so we take time for the first examination.
- **Show all options.** You learn which treatments are possible in your situation – including simpler and less expensive ones.
- **Based on current evidence.** We recommend procedures whose benefit is well supported by research (evidence-based dentistry).
- **Costs agreed in advance.** Before any major treatment you receive a written cost estimate.

## New to the German system?

In Germany, dental costs depend on your insurance. Statutory insurance (gesetzliche Krankenversicherung) covers basic care and pays a fixed subsidy towards crowns, bridges and dentures. Private insurance reimburses according to your policy. Our guide [Seeing a dentist in Berlin](/en/guide/dentist-berlin-german-system/) explains everything step by step.
