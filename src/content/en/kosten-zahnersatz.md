## The fixed subsidy from statutory insurance

For prosthetics, German statutory insurance does not pay a percentage of your bill but a **fixed subsidy (Festzuschuss)**. It depends on your **diagnosis** – what is missing or damaged – and not on the restoration you choose.

For each diagnosis there is a defined **standard solution (Regelversorgung)**: the medically necessary basic option. If, for example, a back tooth is missing between two of your own teeth, the standard solution is usually a metal bridge with tooth-coloured facing in the visible area.

The fixed subsidy is **60 percent** of the average cost of this standard solution.

## The bonus booklet

Regular check-ups pay off:

| Proof | Fixed subsidy |
|---|---|
| no complete bonus booklet | 60 % |
| one check-up every year for five years | 70 % |
| one check-up every year for ten years | 75 % |

For children and teenagers under 18, two check-ups a year count (one per half-year). Have every check-up stamped in your booklet.

## The hardship rule

On low incomes, the insurer pays a **double subsidy** on application, but no more than the cost of the standard solution. If you choose the standard solution, you then pay nothing yourself. If your income is slightly above the limit, a proportionally increased subsidy is possible.

## Same type or different type of restoration

- **Standard solution:** you only pay the difference between subsidy and cost.
- **Same type, more elaborate (gleichartig):** for example an all-ceramic bridge instead of a metal one. You pay the additional cost, partly charged under the German fee scale for dentists (GOZ).
- **Different type (andersartig):** for example an implant instead of a bridge. The insurer pays the fixed subsidy; everything else is charged privately under the GOZ.

## What makes up your own share

Your own share consists of the **dentist’s fee** and **material and lab costs**. Statutory services have fixed prices (BEMA and BEL II); private services follow the GOZ with a multiplier that depends on difficulty and time.

## Reading your treatment and cost plan

Before any prosthetic treatment we prepare a **treatment and cost plan (Heil- und Kostenplan)**. It contains:

1. the diagnosis and the planned restoration
2. the expected costs for fees, material and lab
3. your insurer’s fixed subsidy
4. your expected own share

With statutory insurance you submit the plan to your insurer before treatment. We start after approval.

## Private and supplementary insurance

With private insurance, billing follows the GOZ. How much is reimbursed depends on your policy. Submit the cost plan to your insurer in advance. Supplementary dental policies often have waiting periods and maximum amounts in the first years.

## International patients

The fixed subsidy only applies with German statutory insurance. With an EHIC, planned prosthetics are usually not covered. With international or travel insurance you pay the invoice and claim it back. See [Seeing a dentist in Berlin](/en/guide/dentist-berlin-german-system/).
