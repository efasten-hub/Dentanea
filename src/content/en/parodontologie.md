## How the treatment works

1. **Diagnosis:** we measure the depth of the gum pockets, check bleeding and mobility and take X-rays. We ask about risk factors such as smoking and diabetes.
2. **Explanation and oral hygiene:** you receive personal guidance on how best to clean your teeth and the spaces between them.
3. **Deep cleaning:** under local anaesthetic we remove deposits and bacteria from the gum pockets and smooth the root surfaces.
4. **Re-evaluation:** after about three months we measure again.
5. **Further treatment for deep pockets:** if needed, we consider surgical or regenerative procedures that can partly rebuild lost tissue.
6. **Supportive aftercare:** regular check-ups and cleaning – every three to twelve months depending on risk – keep the result stable.

## Alternatives

There is no equivalent alternative to treatment. Without therapy, periodontitis usually progresses. We use antibiotics only selectively and as a supplement.

## Duration and number of visits

Active treatment usually involves four to six visits over three to six months, followed by long-term aftercare.

## Durability and care

What matters most is your daily oral hygiene, especially cleaning between the teeth, and regular aftercare. Stopping smoking clearly improves healing.

## Costs

- **Statutory insurance (GKV):** the insurer covers systematic periodontitis treatment, including explanation, oral hygiene instruction, pocket cleaning, re-evaluation and supportive aftercare, usually for two years. The treatment must be approved by the insurer beforehand.
- **Private insurance and self-payers:** charged according to the German fee scale for dentists (GOZ).
- **Not covered by statutory insurance:** for example regenerative materials or extra cleanings beyond the covered aftercare. Guide value: [[PREIS]] euros.
