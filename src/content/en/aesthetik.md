## Veneers

Veneers are wafer-thin ceramic shells bonded to the front of the teeth. They change colour, shape and length, close small gaps and correct slight misalignment.

1. **Consultation and photos:** we discuss your wishes and check that teeth and gums are healthy.
2. **Design (wax-up):** our lab models the new tooth shapes.
3. **Preview (mock-up):** we transfer the design to your teeth as a thin resin layer. You see the result in the mirror and can ask for changes.
4. **Preparation:** if necessary, the teeth are minimally prepared and scanned digitally.
5. **Bonding:** the finished veneers are fixed with a special adhesive.

## Whitening

A gel containing hydrogen peroxide lightens the teeth. Beforehand we clean the teeth professionally and check for caries or exposed tooth necks.

- **Home whitening:** you wear custom-made trays with gel at home, usually for one to two weeks.
- **In-office whitening:** we apply a stronger gel in the practice while protecting the gums.
- **Single-tooth whitening:** a tooth that has darkened after root canal treatment is lightened from the inside.

## Aligners

Aligners are clear plastic trays that move the teeth step by step (for example Invisalign). They are barely visible and removable. After a digital scan, the tooth movement is planned on a computer, and you see the planned result in an animation. Every one to two weeks you switch to the next tray. After treatment, a retainer keeps the teeth in place.

## Alternatives

Small changes in shape can often be made with composite, a tooth-coloured filling material – without drilling and less expensive than ceramic, but less colour-stable. Sometimes a professional cleaning is enough to remove stains.

## Duration and number of visits

- Veneers: three to four visits over three to six weeks
- Whitening: one visit in the practice, or two visits plus one to two weeks at home
- Aligners: usually six to eighteen months, check-ups every six to ten weeks

## Durability and care

Ceramic veneers keep their colour and can last many years with good care. Avoid biting very hard objects. If you grind your teeth at night, a splint protects your veneers. After aligner treatment, wearing a retainer is important, otherwise the teeth can move back.

## Costs

Cosmetic treatments are **private services**. German statutory insurance does not pay for them. They are charged according to the German fee scale for dentists (GOZ). Private insurance usually only reimburses medically necessary treatment.
