## Our own lab

Our master dental technician makes crowns, bridges, veneers, resin-bonded bridges and splints right in the practice. Short distances make it easier to match shade and shape – directly at the chair if you wish. See [In-house dental lab & digital dentistry](/en/services/in-house-dental-lab/).

## Equipment

- Intraoral scanner for digital impressions
- CAD/CAM production and 3D implant planning
- Digital X-rays with a low radiation dose
- 3D X-ray imaging (CBCT) [[PRÜFEN: vorhanden?]]

## Hygiene

We follow the recommendations of the Commission for Hospital Hygiene and Infection Prevention (KRINKO) at the Robert Koch Institute. Instruments are cleaned, disinfected and sterilised in validated processes, and every step is documented. [[PRÜFEN: Hygieneangaben]]
