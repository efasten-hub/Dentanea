## Your first appointment

Please bring your health card or proof of insurance, your bonus booklet if you have one and – if available – recent X-rays, reports and a list of your medications. Come five minutes early to fill in the medical history form.

If you cannot attend, please cancel at least 24 hours in advance – online via Doctolib or by phone.

## Emergencies outside opening hours

The Berlin dental emergency service can be found via the Berlin Dental Chamber at [zaek-berlin.de](https://www.zaek-berlin.de). For life-threatening emergencies, call 112. [[PRÜFEN: aktuelle Notdienstnummer Berlin]]
