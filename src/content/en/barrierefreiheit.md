We want everyone to be able to use our website easily – regardless of age, disability or technology.

## Compliance status

The website was designed and tested according to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. This includes:

- sufficient colour contrast and readable font sizes
- full keyboard operation with visible focus
- meaningful alternative text for images
- a clear heading structure, a skip link to the content and descriptive link texts
- touch targets of at least 44 × 44 pixels
- no automatically running animations; the “reduce motion” setting is respected
- language markup for all four language versions

## Known limitations

- **Online booking:** booking takes place on the Doctolib website. We have no influence on its accessibility. You can always book by phone.
- **Map:** the optional OpenStreetMap map is not fully accessible. The address and directions are therefore also given as text.
- **Translations:** some articles are not yet available in all languages.

## Preparation of this statement

This statement was prepared on [[DATUM]], based on a self-assessment with automated tests (axe) and manual checks with keyboard and screen reader.
