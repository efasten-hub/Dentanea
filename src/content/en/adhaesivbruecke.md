## Who is a resin-bonded bridge suitable for?

- When **a single tooth** is missing, especially an incisor
- When the neighbouring teeth are healthy and should not be drilled
- When an implant is not possible or not wanted, for example because bone grafting would be needed
- For **teenagers** whose jaws are still growing – for instance with congenitally missing teeth

The supporting tooth needs to be healthy and large enough with sufficient enamel; gums and bite need to be stable.

## How the treatment works

1. **Examination:** we check the supporting tooth, gums and bite and discuss the alternatives.
2. **Preparation:** if anything, only a very shallow rest is made in the enamel on the back of the supporting tooth. Then we scan the teeth digitally.
3. **Making the bridge:** our lab usually makes it from all-ceramic material and matches shade and shape to the neighbouring teeth.
4. **Bonding:** the bridge is bonded under isolation with a rubber dam. This technique is key to how long it lasts.

## Alternatives

- [Implant](/en/services/dental-implants/): if there is enough bone and the jaw has stopped growing
- Conventional bridge: if the neighbouring teeth need crowns anyway
- Orthodontic gap closure: in some cases for teenagers
- Small partial denture: a simple, low-cost temporary solution

A detailed comparison is in our guide [Implant or resin-bonded bridge?](/en/guide/implant-or-resin-bonded-bridge/)

## Duration and number of visits

Usually two to three visits within two to three weeks. No surgery on the bone is needed.

## Durability and care

Well-planned and carefully bonded bridges can last many years. Do not bite very hard things with your front teeth, such as nutshells. Clean between your teeth daily with floss or interdental brushes. At check-ups we examine the bond.

## Costs

- **Statutory insurance (GKV):** for a missing incisor, a resin-bonded bridge can be the standard solution under certain conditions. The insurer then pays the **fixed subsidy (Festzuschuss)** – 60 percent, with a complete **bonus booklet** 70 or 75 percent of the standard solution. If you choose all-ceramic or a bridge on back teeth, you pay the difference. [[PRÜFEN: Regelversorgung Adhäsivbrücke]]
- **Private insurance and self-payers:** charged according to the German fee scale for dentists (GOZ).

**New to Germany?** Read [Seeing a dentist in Berlin](/en/guide/dentist-berlin-german-system/) for how insurance and costs work here.

Your own share for an all-ceramic resin-bonded bridge is usually around [[PREIS]] euros. Your cost plan shows the exact amount.
