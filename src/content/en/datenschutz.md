Protecting your data matters to us – especially because a dental practice deals with health data. This website therefore works **without cookies, without tracking and without embedded third-party content**. Fonts and images are stored on our own server. No data is transferred to third parties when you open a page.

## 2. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany, on servers in Germany. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place.

Each time a page is accessed, the server automatically stores the following in log files:

- IP address of the requesting device
- date and time of access
- page or file requested and amount of data transferred
- status code (for example “found” or “not found”)
- referring website, if your browser transmits it
- browser type and operating system

We need this data to operate the website securely and reliably and to detect attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure operation). Log files are deleted or anonymised after [[PRÜFEN: Speicherdauer laut Hetzner-Konfiguration, z. B. 7 Tage]]. They are not combined with other data.

The connection is encrypted with TLS (“https://”).

## 3. No cookies, no analytics

We do not use cookies or any analytics or marketing tools. There are no social media plugins. A short notice pointing to another language version of this website is based solely on your browser’s language setting; nothing is stored.

## 4. Contact by phone and email

If you call or email us, we process your details to handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to or performance of the treatment contract) and, where you provide health information, Art. 9(2)(h) GDPR in conjunction with Section 22(1)(1)(b) of the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG). Please do not send findings or X-rays by unencrypted email.

## 5. Request form

Using the request form, you can send us your name, a phone number or email address, a preferred date, your preferred language, a topic and a short message. Only your name and one way of contacting you are required. The language of the page you send the request from is transmitted too, so that we can reply in your language.

- **Purpose:** answering your request and arranging an appointment
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR, where your details allow conclusions about your health. You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.
- **Transmission:** the form is sent encrypted to our server at Hetzner and forwarded from there by email to the practice mailbox. The content is not stored on the web server.
- **Spam protection:** to prevent misuse, a non-reversible hash of your IP address is stored for no more than one hour. An invisible field helps detect automated submissions.
- **Retention:** we delete requests once they have been dealt with and no statutory retention obligations apply. If the request leads to treatment, section 8 applies.

Please do not send findings, X-rays or detailed health information via the form. You are welcome to bring them to your appointment.

## 6. Online booking via Doctolib

We use Doctolib (Doctolib GmbH, Mehringdamm 51, 10961 Berlin, Germany) for online booking. [[PRÜFEN: Anschrift Doctolib]]

Doctolib is only **linked** on our website. No data is transferred to Doctolib when you visit our pages. Only when you click “Book online” does the Doctolib website open; Doctolib’s privacy notice applies there.

If you book via Doctolib, Doctolib processes your details (for example name, contact details, appointment type) on our behalf to manage appointments. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and Art. 9(2)(h) GDPR in conjunction with Section 22 BDSG. [[PRÜFEN: AV-Vertrag mit Doctolib, Rollenverteilung]]

## 7. Map (OpenStreetMap)

On the contact page you can display a map from OpenStreetMap. The map is loaded **only after you click**. Your IP address and technical browser data are then transferred to the OpenStreetMap Foundation (St John’s Innovation Centre, Cowley Road, Cambridge, CB4 0WS, United Kingdom). An adequacy decision of the European Commission exists for the United Kingdom. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, given by clicking. Without a click, no data is transferred.

## 8. Patient data in the practice

During treatment we process health data, i.e. special categories of personal data under Art. 9 GDPR. The legal basis is Art. 9(2)(h) GDPR in conjunction with Section 22(1)(1)(b) BDSG and the treatment contract (Sections 630a et seq. of the German Civil Code, BGB). All staff are bound by medical confidentiality.

We only transfer data where required by law or with your consent, for example to your health insurer, the Association of Statutory Health Insurance Dentists in Berlin (KZV Berlin), an external lab, co-treating doctors or – for private billing – a billing service. [[PRÜFEN: externe Abrechnungsstelle?]]

We keep treatment records for at least ten years after treatment ends (Section 630f BGB), unless other regulations require longer periods.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to

- access your stored data (Art. 15 GDPR)
- rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
- erasure (Art. 17 GDPR), unless a retention obligation applies
- restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
- data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
- object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR)
- withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR)

Please contact us at the address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, [datenschutz-berlin.de](https://www.datenschutz-berlin.de).
