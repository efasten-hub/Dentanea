## Common situations

- **Congenitally missing teeth:** one or more permanent teeth never developed.
- **Peg-shaped teeth:** teeth that are too small or pointed.
- **Tooth loss after an accident:** for example a knocked-out incisor.
- **Severely damaged teeth:** for example with chalky teeth (MIH) or inherited enamel defects – see [MIH and rare conditions](/en/services/mih-chalky-teeth-rare-conditions/).

## Possible solutions

- **Composite build-up:** we reshape peg-shaped teeth and small defects with tooth-coloured resin, without drilling.
- **Resin-bonded bridge:** a missing incisor can be replaced with a single-wing [resin-bonded bridge](/en/services/resin-bonded-bridge/) – gentle and often suitable long term.
- **Children’s denture:** a small removable denture when several teeth are missing, adapted as the child grows.
- **Crowns:** for badly damaged teeth, for example made of resin or ceramic.
- **Orthodontic gap closure:** sometimes better than a replacement – we decide this together with the orthodontist.

## How we proceed

1. **Getting to know each other:** we take time for child and parents, examine playfully and explain everything in a child-friendly way.
2. **Findings:** examination, X-rays if needed, and a digital scan instead of impression paste.
3. **Joint plan:** with you and – if necessary – with orthodontics, paediatrics or human genetics.
4. **Treatment in stages:** interim solutions that grow with the child, up to the final restoration.
5. **Regular check-ups:** we adjust the restoration as your child grows.

## Duration and number of visits

A composite build-up usually takes one to two visits, a resin-bonded bridge two to three. Overall treatment often accompanies your child for several years.

## Care

Children need help brushing until they are about eight. We show you and your child how best to clean the restoration. Fluoride toothpaste and regular prevention protect the teeth.

## Costs

- **Statutory insurance (GKV):** for prosthetics, children also receive the diagnosis-based **fixed subsidy (Festzuschuss)**, increased with a complete **bonus booklet**. Prevention for children aged 6 to 17 is covered. For **generalised, genetically caused missing teeth** or congenital malformations, statutory insurance may exceptionally cover implants. This requires an application with an expert opinion; we support you.
- **Private insurance:** charged according to the German fee scale for dentists (GOZ).
