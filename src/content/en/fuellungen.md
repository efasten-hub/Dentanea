## How the treatment works

1. **Diagnosis:** examination and an X-ray if needed.
2. **Removing decay:** under local anaesthetic we remove only the diseased tissue.
3. **Isolation:** with a rubber dam wherever possible, so that the bond holds reliably.
4. **Layering:** composite is applied in thin layers and cured with light. Different shades create a natural look.
5. **Finishing:** we adjust the filling to your bite and polish it to a high gloss. Smooth surfaces attract less plaque.

## Alternatives

- **Ceramic inlay or partial crown:** for large defects, made in our own lab
- **Glass ionomer cement:** as a temporary filling, for example in baby teeth
- **Monitoring:** for very small early lesions in the enamel, with fluoride and good care

## Duration and number of visits

Usually one visit of 30 to 60 minutes per tooth.

## Durability and care

Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, clean between your teeth and come for regular check-ups. That way we spot small leaks early.

## Costs

- **Statutory insurance (GKV):** since 2025 the insurer covers standard tooth-coloured fillings on back teeth too, for example self-adhesive materials. For an elaborate multi-layer filling you pay the difference (additional costs under the German fee scale for dentists, GOZ), agreed in writing beforehand.
- **Private insurance and self-payers:** charged according to the GOZ.
