## Conditions

- **Chalky teeth (MIH):** a mineralisation disorder of the first permanent molars and often the incisors. In the Fifth German Oral Health Study (DMS V), almost 30 percent of 12-year-olds showed signs of MIH, mostly mild. [[PRÜFEN: aktuelle Zahl aus DMS 6]]
- **Amelogenesis imperfecta:** an inherited enamel disorder, usually affecting all teeth
- **Dentinogenesis imperfecta:** an inherited dentine disorder
- **Peg-shaped and missing teeth:** on their own or as part of a syndrome, such as ectodermal dysplasia

## How we proceed

1. **Diagnosis and risk assessment:** we examine how badly the teeth are affected, how sensitive they are and how high the risk of decay is.
2. **Relieve pain and protect:** fluoride varnishes, desensitising agents and sealants make the teeth more resistant.
3. **Build up:** we restore defects with layered composite, without drilling where possible.
4. **Cover:** badly damaged molars are protected with partial crowns or crowns – depending on age made of resin, metal or ceramic.
5. **Appearance:** discoloured incisors can be improved with composite, later with veneers.
6. **Teamwork:** for syndromes and complex findings we work with orthodontics, paediatrics and human genetics.

## For parents

Children with sensitive teeth are often afraid of treatment. We take our time, explain every step and keep appointments short. Good pain control is especially important – affected teeth are sometimes harder to numb. We plan for this.

Dr. Adali wrote a chapter on children’s dentistry in the German handbook of paediatric primary care (“Praxishandbuch der pädiatrischen Grundversorgung”, Elsevier, 2024) and received the 2020 practitioner award of the German Society of Paediatric Dentistry (Praktikerpreis der Deutschen Gesellschaft für Kinderzahnmedizin, DGKiZ).

## Duration and number of visits

Treatment takes place in stages and often accompanies your child for years. Individual appointments usually last 30 to 60 minutes.

## Care

Brush with fluoride toothpaste in the amount and strength recommended for your child’s age. For sensitive teeth, lukewarm water and a soft toothbrush help. Regular check-ups, for example every three months, are important.

## Costs

- **Statutory insurance (GKV):** prevention for children aged 6 to 17, fluoride, sealants and fillings are largely covered. For crowns and prosthetics the insurer pays the diagnosis-based **fixed subsidy (Festzuschuss)**, increased with a complete **bonus booklet**. Elaborate cosmetic restorations such as veneers may involve additional costs under the German fee scale for dentists (GOZ).
- **Private insurance:** charged according to the GOZ.
