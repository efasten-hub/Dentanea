faq_gruppen:
  - titel: "Appointments"
    fragen:
      - frage: "Can I book an appointment online?"
        antwort: "Yes. You can book via Doctolib at any time, also as a new patient. The booking page is available in English. Choose the type of appointment, such as first examination, check-up, cleaning or implant consultation, and you will receive a confirmation by email."
      - frage: "What if there is no suitable appointment online?"
        antwort: "Call us on +49 30 44 26 84 3. By phone we can often offer appointments that are not available online. Alternatively, write to us using the [request form](/en/appointment-contact/#anfrage); we usually reply within two working days."
      - frage: "Which appointments are better booked by phone?"
        antwort: "A second opinion, a first consultation for complex cases and appointments for children with special findings, such as missing teeth or inherited dental conditions. We then plan enough time."
      - frage: "What should I do if I have acute toothache?"
        antwort: "Call us during opening hours. Online you can choose the appointment type for acute complaints/emergencies. Outside opening hours, the Berlin dental emergency service can help."
      - frage: "How do I cancel an appointment?"
        antwort: "Online via Doctolib or by phone, please at least 24 hours in advance."
      - frage: "What should I bring to my first appointment?"
        antwort: "Your health card or proof of insurance, your bonus booklet if you have one, a list of your medications and – if available – recent X-rays and reports."
  - titel: "Costs and insurance"
    fragen:
      - frage: "Do you treat patients with statutory insurance?"
        antwort: "Yes. We treat patients with statutory and private insurance as well as self-paying patients."
      - frage: "How do I know what a treatment costs?"
        antwort: "Before any larger treatment you receive a written cost plan. For prosthetics this is the treatment and cost plan (Heil- und Kostenplan), which patients with statutory insurance submit to their insurer."
      - frage: "What is the bonus booklet for?"
        antwort: "For prosthetics, the statutory insurer’s fixed subsidy rises from 60 to 70 percent after five years and to 75 percent after ten years of regular check-ups. More in [What do crowns, bridges and dentures cost?](/en/guide/cost-of-dental-prosthetics/)"
      - frage: "Does insurance pay for professional teeth cleaning?"
        antwort: "Statutory insurance usually does not pay in full; many insurers give a subsidy. For children and teenagers aged 6 to 17, individual prevention is covered."
  - titel: "Treatment"
    fragen:
      - frage: "Do you offer second opinions?"
        antwort: "Yes. Bring your X-rays, reports and cost plans. We look at everything carefully and explain our assessment."
      - frage: "I am afraid of the dentist. What can I do?"
        antwort: "Let us know when booking. We take more time, explain every step and agree on a hand signal so you can pause at any time."
      - frage: "Do you treat children?"
        antwort: "Yes, especially children and teenagers with missing or weakened teeth, such as congenitally missing teeth, peg-shaped teeth or chalky teeth (MIH)."
      - frage: "Do you have your own lab?"
        antwort: "Yes. Our master dental technician makes restorations right in the practice. See [In-house dental lab](/en/services/in-house-dental-lab/)."
  - titel: "Languages and international patients"
    fragen:
      - frage: "Which languages can I be treated in?"
        antwort: "Dr. Adali advises and treats patients in English, German, Spanish and Turkish."
      - frage: "I am not insured in Germany. Can I still come?"
        antwort: "Yes. With a European Health Insurance Card (EHIC), necessary treatment is generally billed like statutory insurance. Without German or European insurance, we treat you as a self-paying patient under the German fee scale for dentists (GOZ), and you receive an invoice for your insurer. Read [Seeing a dentist in Berlin](/en/guide/dentist-berlin-german-system/)."
