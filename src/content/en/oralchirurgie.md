## What we offer

- **Extractions:** gentle, with as little bone loss as possible – important if an implant is planned later
- **Wisdom teeth:** removal including impacted or deep-lying teeth
- **Apicoectomy:** removal of an inflamed root tip if root canal treatment alone is not enough
- **Exposure:** of teeth for orthodontics or of implants
- **Cysts:** surgical removal with tissue examination
- **Lip and tongue ties:** correction if they are too short
- **Bone grafting and sinus lift:** preparation for implants
- **Pre-prosthetic surgery:** preparing the jaw for a well-fitting denture

## How a procedure works

1. **Diagnosis:** examination and X-ray, in 3D (CBCT) if needed. [[PRÜFEN: DVT in der Praxis oder Überweisung?]]
2. **Information:** we explain the procedure, risks, alternatives and costs. You have time for your questions.
3. **Procedure:** under local anaesthetic. If needed, we discuss sedation. [[PRÜFEN: Sedierung angeboten?]]
4. **Aftercare:** check-up after a few days, stitches removed after about a week.

## Alternatives

Many teeth can be saved, for example with root canal treatment. Instead of bone grafting, shorter implants or a resin-bonded bridge may be options. We show you what is possible in your situation.

## Duration and healing

Simple procedures take 20 to 60 minutes. Soft tissue heals in one to two weeks; bone grafts need three to six months before an implant is placed or loaded.

## Costs

- **Statutory insurance (GKV):** extractions, wisdom tooth surgery, apicoectomies and cyst operations are covered. Bone grafting and sinus lifts in connection with implants are usually private services.
- **Private insurance and self-payers:** charged according to the German fee scale for dentists (GOZ).
