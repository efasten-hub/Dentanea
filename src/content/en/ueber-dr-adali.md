## Approach

Good dentistry starts with listening and a thorough examination. I explain findings so that you understand them and show you all sensible options – including the simpler ones. My recommendations are based on current scientific evidence. Where there are several good ways forward, you decide with me.

## What the qualifications mean

**Spezialist für Prothetik (DGPro):** the German Society of Prosthetic Dentistry and Biomaterials (Deutsche Gesellschaft für Prothetische Zahnmedizin und Biomaterialien) awards this title after several years of structured postgraduate training, documented treatment cases and an examination. It is comparable to a prosthodontics specialisation. [[PRÜFEN: Vergabekriterien DGPro]]

**Zertifizierter Implantologe (DGI):** the German Society of Implantology (Deutsche Gesellschaft für Implantologie) certifies proven training and experience in implant dentistry. [[PRÜFEN: Vergabekriterien DGI]]
