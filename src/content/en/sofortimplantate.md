## Terms

- **Immediate placement:** the implant is placed directly after a tooth is removed, in the same socket.
- **Immediate restoration:** the implant receives a temporary crown or bridge on the same day, without full chewing load.
- **Immediate loading:** the temporary restoration is loaded straight away, usually with several splinted implants in an edentulous jaw.
- **Conventional approach:** the implant heals for two to four months without load before the restoration follows.

## Who is it suitable for?

- If there is enough firm bone for the implants to be stable immediately (primary stability)
- If there is no acute inflammation at the site
- With good general health; smoking and poorly controlled diabetes increase the risk
- In edentulous jaws, often with four to six implants carrying a fixed bridge

## Procedure

1. **Examination and 3D diagnostics:** we check bone, gums and general health.
2. **Digital planning:** implant positions and the shape of the temporary teeth are planned in advance; the temporary is prepared in the lab.
3. **Treatment day:** remove teeth if necessary, place implants, fix the temporary.
4. **Healing:** soft food, good care, check-ups.
5. **Final restoration:** after three to six months.

## Advantages and limits

**Advantages:** no time without teeth, fewer procedures, quick return to everyday life.

**Limits:** not possible for everyone. The procedure is more complex and planning more demanding. If the implants are not stable enough on the day, we switch to the step-by-step approach during the procedure. We discuss this with you beforehand.

## Costs

German statutory insurance usually only pays the **fixed subsidy** for the restoration (increased with a complete **bonus booklet**), not for the implants themselves. Implant surgery, temporary and planning are charged under the German fee scale for dentists (GOZ). Guide value for a fixed bridge on four implants per jaw: [[PREIS]] euros. You receive a detailed cost plan in advance. For international patients, see [Seeing a dentist in Berlin](/en/guide/dentist-berlin-german-system/).
