## How the cleaning works

1. **Check:** we examine teeth and gums and, if useful, stain plaque to make it visible.
2. **Removing deposits:** with ultrasound and fine hand instruments we remove tartar and plaque above and – if needed – just below the gum line.
3. **Powder jet:** a fine powder gently removes stains and bacterial plaque.
4. **Between the teeth:** we clean the spaces between your teeth thoroughly.
5. **Polishing and fluoride:** smooth surfaces attract less plaque; fluoride strengthens the enamel.
6. **Care tips:** we show you which toothbrush and interdental brushes suit you.

## Especially important for

- Implants, bridges or crowns
- After periodontitis treatment
- Diabetes, pregnancy or smoking
- Fixed braces or aligners

## Duration

A cleaning usually takes 45 to 60 minutes.

## Costs

- **Statutory insurance (GKV):** professional cleaning is usually a private service. Many insurers pay a subsidy – ask yours. Once a year, removal of tartar is covered. For children and teenagers aged 6 to 17, individual prevention is covered.
- **Private insurance:** most policies reimburse professional cleaning, sometimes up to a limit.
- **Supplementary dental insurance:** many policies pay once or twice a year.
